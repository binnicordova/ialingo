What we heard

The brief

A voice-first English app for kids aged 6 to 10, Spanish and Portuguese speakers first. Pets hatch and grow as a child learns. No login, no ads, no data leaves the phone. The logo has to be cute enough for a child to love, trustworthy enough for a parent to install, and instantly different from the green owl in the category.

cuteplayfulchunkysafemagical

Success: reads as a pet and as the letter L, survives on a 16 px tab, works in one colour, sits naturally beside the app's violet and Fredoka type.

Final design

Lingosaur

A baby dino whose whole body is the capital L of Lingo.

  • The mark is a letter and a pet at once, so the name and the game's promise arrive together
  • Yellow on violet stands apart from the category's green and blue, and comes straight from the app's own tokens
  • In the logotype the pet takes the place of the L, which no typed name can copy
  • Simplified 16 to 24 px cut keeps L plus two eyes on browser tabs
