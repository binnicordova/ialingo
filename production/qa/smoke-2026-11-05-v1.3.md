Français"), then the whole app in French: home, quests, lessons (learn, story, song, speaking), results and celebrations, Word Gym, sticker album, Play Park, pets, guides, badges, certificates, parent gate and dashboard, a second player, "Qui joue ?", bedtime.

- A new world (**Les mots magiques**) and its sing-along in French.

## Defects found and fixed during QA

1. **Disabled, selected and checked states never reached the browser**: react-native-web ignores `accessibilityState` and derives `aria-disabled` from the `Pressable`'s `disabled` prop. Wrong answers in Bubble Pop, I Spy and Odd One Out now set `disabled`, and selected/checked use `aria-*` props. This also fixes screen-reader state on web, and the QA loops that silently stalled after a wrong answer.
2. **Odd One Out lock message** said "learn 6 words" when the child had only met words from mixed worlds. It now says "learn words in 2 worlds".
3. **French album tile title** was cut off ("Album d'autocolla…"). Renamed "Mes autocollants".

## Not verifiable in this environment

- A native-speaker review of the French content (recommended before launch in France and Québec).
- Everything listed in the v1.2 report (speech recognition, recordings and store submission on real devices).
