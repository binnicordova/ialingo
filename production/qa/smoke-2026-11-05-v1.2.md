## Defects found and fixed during QA

1. **Mini-game payout inside a React state updater** caused "Cannot update a component while rendering a different component" (CelebrationOverlay). The payout is now computed in the timer callback before `setGame`.
2. **Missing-word exercise gave the answer away:** the line's picture matched the answer tile. It now shows 🎶 until answered.
3. **Activity tile titles were cut off** in Spanish ("Gimnasi…"). Titles wrap to two lines.
4. **Spaced-repetition inflation (since v1.0):** repeated correct answers on the same day promoted a word up to "mastered". Boxes now move only on due reviews (ADR 0006).
5. **Kids Category gap:** the microphone permission prompt was not behind the parental gate. It is now, for speaking and voice postcards.
6. **Home hero title (since v1.1)** showed "Boss" for story and speaking lessons. It now uses the shared lesson titles.

## Not verifiable in this environment

- Speech recognition, the Android offline model download and voice recording on physical devices (the adapter is covered by tests against a fake native module).
- Chant timing with real TTS voices (the web build in headless Chromium has no voices; the safety timer moves the song forward).
- Store submission: `eas metadata:push` needs App Store Connect credentials and a public privacy policy URL (see `store/README.md`).
